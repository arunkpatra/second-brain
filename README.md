# SecondBrain (working title)

A wearable AI note-taker for professionals who spend the day in meetings, built for Indian users first. A small capsule captures conversations, a phone app syncs them, and a cloud backend turns them into daily and weekly summaries.

**Status:** hobby project. Concept design only. No hardware built yet.

## Target design in brief

- 45.3 × 28 × 9 mm, under 18 g. The face is a golden rectangle.
- Radii from one series: 4.1 mm face corners, 2.5 mm edges.
- Always-on capture gated by voice activity, a mute slider, a mark button and a recording LED.
- Magnetic charging with no open port, sealed to IP54 or better.

Every figure is a starting estimate until the four prototype units are built and tested.
