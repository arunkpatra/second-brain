SecondBrain Tab

A slim capture capsule for people in meetings all day. Always ready, visibly recording, worn three ways.

Size
45.3 × 28 × 9 mm
Weight
Under 18 g
Battery target
16 hours
Sealing
IP54 or better
Design principles
  • State you can feel. A mute slider, not a press toggle.
  • Honest to bystanders. A front LED is lit while capturing.
  • No ports. Magnetic charging keeps it sealed and thin.
  • One core, three carriers. Pendant, clip, lanyard badge.
  • Quiet object. Matte graphite, no logo on the front.
  • Golden proportions. A 28 × 45.3 face, with every radius from the same series.
All dimensions and performance figures are starting estimates, to be tested on the four prototype units.
