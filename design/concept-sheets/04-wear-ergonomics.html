Wear ergonomics

Sheet 04 · Figure at 0.8 px per mm · Distances estimated
Audio
Closer is better. The top mic aims at the wearer, the front mic at the room. Two mics let software separate the two.
Orientation
Every carrier holds the Tab upright, face out. A flipped device points its mics and LED at the chest.
Reach
The slider sits on the right edge, under a right thumb. The mark button is a flat press with the palm or two fingers, with no need to look down.
Feedback
One short buzz on mark. Two on mute. The wearer never needs to see the LED, which is for other people.
Comfort
Under 18 g, with 2.5 mm edge radii against skin and fabric. No sharp corner to catch a dupatta or a seat belt.
Heat and sweat
Sealed shell and mesh-covered mics. The pendant frame keeps a small air gap between the Tab and skin.
To test on the prototypes: speech pickup at each of the three positions, in a car, a café and under a ceiling fan.
