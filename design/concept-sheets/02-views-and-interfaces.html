Interfaces
  1. Microphone 1. Front slot behind a sealed mesh. Faces the other speaker.
  2. Recording LED. Lit while capturing, off when muted. Visible from 2 m.
  3. Mark button. 17.3 mm dish, centred in the lower square. One press flags the moment.
  4. Mute slider. 5 mm travel on the flat side band. Orange shows when muted.
  5. Carrier mount. Magnet ring on the back. Snaps into any carrier in one orientation.
  6. Charging pads. Two gold contacts for a magnetic cradle. No open port.
  7. Microphone 2. Top edge, pointing up at the wearer's mouth.
Proportions
The face is a golden rectangle, 28 × 45.3. The dashed line splits it into a 17.3 band and a 28 square. Radii come from the same series: 4.1 on face corners, 2.5 on edges, which leaves a 4 mm flat band for the slider.
Not shown: haptic motor, antenna along the top edge. The 4.0 mm cell bay suggests roughly 200 to 250 mAh. Every figure is an estimate until components are chosen.
