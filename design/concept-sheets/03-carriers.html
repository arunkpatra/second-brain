One core, three carriers

Sheet 03 · The Tab snaps into each by magnet

Pendant

  • Thin metal frame on an adjustable cord.
  • Sits on the sternum, outside the top layer.
  • Best with a T-shirt, kurta or round neck.
  • Weak point: slips under a dupatta or jacket.

Magnetic clip

  • A thin plate behind the fabric holds the Tab.
  • Placket, pocket edge, collar or lapel.
  • Closest to the mouth, so the best audio.
  • Weak point: thick fabric weakens the hold.

Lanyard badge mount

  • Sits where the strap meets the ID card.
  • Nobody looks twice at a lanyard in an office.
  • Fits a standard 20 mm strap and hook.
  • Weak point: farthest from the mouth, and it swings.
